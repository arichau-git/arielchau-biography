Deloitte's Business Chemistry is a science-based framework that identifies four working styles — Pioneer, Driver, Integrator, and Guardian — to help people understand how they think, work, and engage with others. My primary type is Driver, someone who values challenge and generates momentum by moving fast and direct toward results; my secondary type is Guardian, bringing order, rigor, and a practical, detail-oriented approach.

Volunteer & Community

Giving back.

Non-profit organizations I have supported.

  • University of Minnesota Coaching Program — Career Mentor
    University of Minnesota Career Services mentoring programs connect students with alumni and industry professionals who offer career guidance, insights, and support as they prepare for the workforce.
  • Minnesota Animal Humane Society — Volunteer
    Serving Minnesota since 1878, the Animal Humane Society is a nonprofit dedicated to engaging the community to help animals through adoption, veterinary care, and animal welfare advocacy.
  • Global Education Opportunities Showcase — Volunteer
    The annual Global Education Showcase is dedicated to recognizing the outstanding achievements and hard work of both DCPS students and teachers in global education.
  • World AIDS Day — Volunteer
    Observed annually on December 1 since 1988, World AIDS Day unites people around the world to raise awareness about HIV/AIDS, support those living with HIV, and remember those who have died from AIDS-related illnesses.
  • Operation Track Shoes — Volunteer
    Operation Trackshoes was an annual sports festival held at the University of Victoria that gave British Columbians with developmental disabilities the chance to compete in track, field, and swim events in a supportive, inclusive environment.
  • The Big Give — Volunteer
    The Big Give is a holiday charity event where teams take part in hands-on giving activities, building donated items like plush toys, wagons, and blankets for children and families in need.
  • UStrive — Volunteer
    UStrive is a nonprofit that connects low-income, first-generation high school students with free, one-on-one virtual mentors to support them through college admissions, financial aid, and scholarship applications.
