I spent most of my years helping organizations make sense of their data, architecting pipelines, building dashboards, and running the analytics behind decisions that affect real teams and real budgets. Most of my work lives at the intersection of technical delivery and stakeholder trust, translating what a dataset says into something an executive can act on, and translating what a client needs into something an engineering team can build.

Before Deloitte, I spent a year in healthcare working directly with customers, and it gave me an experience that shaped how I think about analytics: behind every dataset is a person making a decision, and good insight should make that decision easier, not just more sophisticated.

Outside of client work, I mentor students navigating their first job search, volunteer at the animal shelter, and look for any excuse to see the world. When budget doesn't allow, you'll find me at home playing video games and hanging out with my two cats.
